II

The goal

What Stark is for, in order.

I

Tools people own

Offline. Private. Paid once. LIVE

Stark builds software that runs on your own machine. 15 of the 19 products on sale are browser apps that still work with the plug pulled. Their code holds nothing that sends your data, so Stark cannot read it. 17 products are paid once, with no subscription. Every browser app can be tried free first.

II

Protection

Apex Protocol. The flagship. In development. BUILDING

Cultivate creation. Never overcome. What exists today is the eleven promises, each with a test a stranger can run in a minute. What is being built is the shield around them: community alerts for floods, fires, storms, heat and outbreaks, planned to travel by radio or mesh when the network falls silent; resilience kits; crisis coordination for relief teams. Warden, the first piece of digital protection, is live.

III

Families

Stark Return Home. The second flagship. A horizon. HORIZON

A home where a parent stands close when the child wakes, and close again when the child comes back through the door. A family that chose it and can afford it. And the people who care for children for a living carried across the change: months of cover, a hand with every application, nobody dropped. This is a direction, not a product. IV

Giving

Stark exists to earn so that it can give. HORIZON

The owner's directive: send as much of Stark's profit as it can to non-profit work for the poor, the sick and the broken. That includes small homes for people living with mental illness. That work is Stark Mend the Mind. It is a cause, never a product. It is never sold, never priced, and never a reason given to buy anything.

No money has moved yet. When it does, the amount, the date and the recipient go on a public ledger.
